Where marketing meets the warehouse.

I work in the gap most teams struggle with: the space between the marketing tools that generate data and the decisions that data is supposed to inform.

On the engineering side, I model raw event and ad-platform data into a clean, trustworthy warehouse — pipelines, transformations, and tests that people can rely on. On the analytics side, I turn that foundation into attribution, experimentation, and forecasting that changes how budget gets allocated.

The result is a single source of truth that finance, growth, and leadership can all point at — and act on.
